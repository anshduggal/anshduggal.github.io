About

BEng (Hons) Mechatronics & Robotics — final year graduate focused on building control systems, wearable rehabilitation devices, and embedded applications. My final-year dissertation was an upper-limb rehabilitation exoskeleton for elbow and wrist flexion movements — hardware design, control, and embedded integration. I enjoy prototyping with Arduinos, stepper/servo motors, and applying machine learning for motion prediction and user intent estimation.
